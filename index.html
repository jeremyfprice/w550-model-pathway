The Planner Path

A model interactive pathway for EDUC-W 550. This prototype shows how a learner might move through a topic by making choices, encountering consequences, and revising decisions along the way.

Audience: Middle school learners
Topic: Time management and organization
Form: Choose-your-own-adventure style
Scene 1

This model is intentionally short enough to show the structure clearly. A student project would expand this logic with more passages, more depth, and topic-specific content.
